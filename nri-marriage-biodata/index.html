NRI Marriage Biodata: What Families Abroad Should Include

For NRI families in the US, UK, Canada, the Gulf or Australia, a biodata has to answer extra questions straight away: Where do they live? Will they move? How do we reach them across time zones?

Extra fields for NRI biodatas

  • Current country and city
  • Citizenship or visa status, stated simply (for example “Indian citizen, work visa holder”). Share only what you are comfortable sharing.
  • Willingness to relocate: “Open to discussion” is a perfectly good answer
  • Native place in India
  • Contact details for both places, with a best time to call in IST

Typical structure

  1. Photo and name
  2. Personal details, including location and visa or citizenship status
  3. Education, including degrees from abroad
  4. Career
  5. Family, including where parents and siblings live
  6. About
  7. Partner expectations, including thoughts on where to settle
  8. Contacts in India and abroad

Common mistakes

  • Vague location (“abroad”). Name the city.
  • Leaving out relocation plans, which is often the first question families ask.
  • Listing only an overseas number that elders in India cannot easily call.
  • Over-sharing immigration paperwork details.

Privacy tips before you forward it

  • Show the city, not your home address.
  • Give one parent’s number rather than the candidate’s.
  • Show income as a range, or “well settled”, if you prefer.
  • Keep a photo-free copy for wider forwarding.

Frequently asked questions

Should I mention my visa type? Only at the level you are comfortable with, for example ‘work visa holder’ or ‘permanent resident’. Details can be shared later in conversation.

Which phone number should I give? One number in India (often a parent’s) and one abroad, with a best time to call in IST.

Should elders in India get a different version? Usually the same English biodata works. Many families simply print it for elders. If anyone needs it in another language, a relative can translate it from the finished English text.

Final checklist before sharing

  • Names spelled as in official documents
  • Date, time and place of birth double-checked
  • Horoscope or community fields copied exactly (if used)
  • Phone number tested
  • No complexion words or superlatives
  • PDF under 2 MB, A4, readable when printed in black and white
  • A photo-free copy saved for wider sharing
