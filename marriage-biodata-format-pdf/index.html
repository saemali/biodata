Marriage Biodata Format PDF: Getting a Print-Ready File

A biodata usually lives two lives: it gets printed for elders and the pandit or matchmaker, and it gets forwarded as a file. A good PDF works for both.

Technical checklist

  • Page size: A4 portrait. It prints at any local shop.
  • Length: one page, or two with an optional horoscope or family-tree page.
  • File size: keep it under 2 MB so it opens quickly on phones and sends easily.
  • Fonts: use clear fonts and embed them in the PDF, so regional scripts don’t turn into boxes on other phones.
  • Text size: at least 10 pt. Elders will thank you.
  • Contrast: dark text on a light background, and check it still reads when printed in black and white.
  • Photo: one clear, recent photo, cropped neatly. Avoid heavy filters.

Section checklist

  1. Header or invocation (optional)
  2. Photo and name
  3. Personal details
  4. Horoscope details (optional)
  5. Education and career
  6. Family details
  7. About
  8. Partner expectations
  9. Contact person

Common PDF mistakes

  • Scanned photos of a printout: blurry and hard to read. Export directly from the editor instead.
  • Huge file sizes from uncompressed photos.
  • Missing fonts, which show Hindi, Marathi or Gujarati text as boxes.
  • Editable Word files sent to strangers, where anyone can change the details. Share a PDF, and keep the DOCX for your own edits.
  • Different versions in circulation: name files clearly, for example “Riya_Sharma_Biodata_Oct2026.pdf” (fictional).

Keep two versions

Many families keep: 1. A full version with photo and horoscope, for families you are seriously talking to. 2. A privacy version with no photo and only the city, for wider sharing.

Privacy tips before you forward it

  • Show the city, not your home address.
  • Give one parent’s number rather than the candidate’s.
  • Show income as a range, or “well settled”, if you prefer.
  • Keep a photo-free copy for wider forwarding.

Final checklist before sharing

  • Names spelled as in official documents
  • Date, time and place of birth double-checked
  • Horoscope or community fields copied exactly (if used)
  • Phone number tested
  • No complexion words or superlatives
  • PDF under 2 MB, A4, readable when printed in black and white
  • A photo-free copy saved for wider sharing
