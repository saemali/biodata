Marathi Biodata Format for Marriage

Marathi biodatas (विवाह परिचय पत्र) commonly list गोत्र, कुळ, कुलदैवत, मूळगाव and मामाचे कुळ, with रास, नक्षत्र + चरण, गण, नाडी and मंगळ in the horoscope part. Some families, mainly Maratha, Kunbi and Mali, also add देवक; it is optional. You can hide the horoscope rows. Labels are in English for now; Marathi/Gujarati labels are coming soon.

Field names follow common practice; community-specific conventions are being reviewed by native speakers. Include only what your family is comfortable sharing.
