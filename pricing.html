Questionnaire

  1. Who is filling this in (self, parent, sibling)?
  2. Religion, community (optional), language wanted
  3. Full name, date of birth, height; time & place of birth only if you want horoscope details
  4. Education and work (employer and income optional)
  5. Family: parents' occupations, siblings
  6. Hobbies, values, what a normal weekend looks like
  7. What you hope for in a partner
  8. Contact person and how they prefer to be reached
  9. 1–2 photos if wanted (basic crop only, no face editing)
  10. Deadline

We never invent qualifications, income or horoscope details. Copy horoscope fields exactly from your kundli/jathagam. Client data and photos are deleted 30 days after delivery.
